# Privacy Policy: Home Assistant Google Integration

**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

## Purpose

This is a private, noncommercial integration between the website owner's Home Assistant installation and authorized Google services, including Google Nest and Google Calendar.

## Information accessed

Depending on which integrations the owner enables and authorizes, Home Assistant may access device information, device states, and controls from Google Nest, and calendar event information from Google Calendar. Access is limited by the permissions granted through Google OAuth.

## How information is used

Information is used solely to provide personal home automation, device control, and calendar-based scheduling. The integration is not offered to other users and does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

## Storage and sharing

Integration credentials, tokens, configuration, and any cached service data may be stored in the owner's Home Assistant environment as needed for the integration to operate. Google user data is not intentionally shared with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data necessarily passes through Google and any services configured by the owner to operate Home Assistant.

## Retention and deletion

Stored integration data and OAuth credentials may remain until the integration is removed or its stored configuration is deleted. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account security settings and remove the corresponding integration from Home Assistant.

## Security

Access to the Home Assistant environment is controlled by the owner. No OAuth client secrets, access tokens, or device credentials are published on this website.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact information provided on the [main website](/).

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the integration's features or data handling change.
